# Quality check

Before delivery, verify all of the following:

- Every requirement has a job-description source ID.
- Every `proven`, `transferable`, or `missing` classification cites candidate evidence or an explicit user statement.
- `not_shown` is never described as proof of inability.
- Hard gates are separate from the evidence index.
- No metric, credential, employer, date, result, or level of responsibility was invented.
- The score is labeled as an evidence index and rounded to the nearest five.
- Coverage and confidence appear beside the index.
- The report contains no hiring probability or guaranteed outcome.
- Risk signals are framed as questions, with no protected-trait inference.
- The recommendation follows from the evidence and the user's constraints.
- Tailoring advice uses only supported keywords and claims.
- Private contact details and source documents are not copied into public output.
