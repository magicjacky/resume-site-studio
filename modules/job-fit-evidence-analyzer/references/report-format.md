# Report format

## 1. Decision brief

- Role and organization
- Recommended action: `apply`, `apply_with_focus`, `clarify_first`, or `skip_due_to_confirmed_gate`
- Evidence band and rounded index
- Evidence coverage and confidence
- One-paragraph rationale with requirement IDs

## 2. Hard gates

| Requirement | Status | Evidence | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

List this section even when no hard gate is present. Say whether the posting stated none or the source was unclear.

## 3. Evidence map

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Status | Candidate evidence | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |

Use source IDs in the evidence column. Keep `not_shown`, `missing`, and `unknown` distinct.

## 4. Strongest fit

Explain the two to four most decision-relevant matches. Link each statement to requirement and candidate source IDs.

## 5. Gaps and questions

Separate:

- proven gaps that may require learning or a different role;
- material that may exist but is absent from the resume;
- unclear wording in the job description;
- focused questions that could change the recommendation.

## 6. Application strategy

- Evidence to move higher in the resume or portfolio
- Truthful wording to make more specific
- Projects or examples to prepare for interview discussion
- Keywords supported by real experience
- Claims that must remain omitted until verified

## 7. Source ledger

List the analyzed files, URLs, or user statements and the ID ranges assigned to them. Do not reproduce private personal data in the final report.
